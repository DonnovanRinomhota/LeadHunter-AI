# 1. Product overview

## Problem

SpiritusTec needs a repeatable way to identify organisations that are actively looking for help with automation, AI, websites, integrations and data systems. Manual prospect research is time-consuming, inconsistent and difficult to track.

## Proposed solution

LeadHunter AI collects opportunities from authorised sources and manual submissions, converts them into a common lead record, evaluates the buying signal and fit with SpiritusTec, then prepares an outreach draft for human review.

It is an **opportunity intelligence and sales-assistance system**, not a bulk messaging or indiscriminate scraping tool.

## Primary users

- SpiritusTec sales / business development
- Technical team members who validate service fit
- Project administrators

## Initial target customers

Start with a narrow segment agreed by the team. Candidate segments include SMEs and growing organisations in Southern Africa that have visible needs around:
- Repetitive manual processes and operational automation
- Customer support and enquiry handling
- Website rebuilds or maintenance
- Data consolidation, reporting and integrations
- Knowledge search and internal AI assistants

Do not assume a prospect is a good fit based only on company size or industry. Require evidence of a relevant problem or buying signal.

## Outcomes

- More qualified opportunities discovered per week
- Less time spent researching and qualifying prospects
- Higher-quality, contextual first-contact drafts
- Clear pipeline visibility
- Measurable conversion from opportunity to revenue

## Success metrics

Track:
- Leads discovered
- Percentage passing qualification
- Human acceptance rate of AI-qualified leads
- Draft approval rate
- Outreach response rate
- Meetings booked
- Proposals issued
- Clients won and revenue attributed

The main success metric is **qualified opportunities converted into real business**, not the number of records collected.
