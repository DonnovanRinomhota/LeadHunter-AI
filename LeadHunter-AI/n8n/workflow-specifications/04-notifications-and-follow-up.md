# Workflow 04 — notifications and follow-up

- Send internal Telegram notifications for new high-priority leads, draft reviews and workflow failures.
- Include lead ID, score, service match and dashboard URL; avoid copying unnecessary personal data into chat.
- Create reminders for human follow-up based on pipeline status.
- Follow-up messages remain suggestions until a human reviews them.
- Respect opt-outs and suppress further reminders / outreach where required.
